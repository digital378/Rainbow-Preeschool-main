## 7. SEO Infrastructure

- **robots.txt**: `client/public/robots.txt` — allows all crawlers; disallows /ad*, /ris*, /flyer, /gsc, /api/, WordPress legacy paths, UTM-tracked URLs.
- **sitemap.xml**: generated dynamically by the server; contains the URLs listed above.
- **llms.txt**: `client/public/llms.txt` — concise AI-search summary with canonical-page-owners table.
- **llms-full.txt**: extended full-content variant for AI crawlers.
- **Bot SSR**: pre-rendered HTML with full meta tags, JSON-LD (`EducationalOrganization`, `LocalBusiness`, `WebSite`, `FAQPage`, `Article`, `BreadcrumbList`), and semantic content served to 20+ bot user-agents.
- **Title guard** (`scripts/check-no-title-cannibalisation.ts`): enforces (1) banned soft-marketing words, (2) ≤65 char limit, (3) keyword ownership, (4) SSR↔client title parity. Runs on pre-commit, pre-push, and predeploy.
- **Soft-word body guard** (`scripts/check-soft-marketing-words.ts`): warn-only sweep across body copy.
- **Brand colour guard** (`scripts/check-no-pink.ts`): blocks pink in CSS/inline styles.
