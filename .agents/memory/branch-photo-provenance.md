Do not identify a photo as taken at a specific centre unless its location is independently verified. A real Rainbow Preschool image may be suitable for a centre page without proving it was photographed at that centre; use location-neutral descriptions and a "Thane photo" classification when provenance is unknown.

**Why:** Photos selected for the Kalwa branch page were real Rainbow photos, but none came with evidence tying them to Kalwa. Naming them as Kalwa photos would make an unsupported claim even if their content and alt text were otherwise accurate.

**How to apply:** Check the visible image subject before writing alt text, confirm content-hash exclusivity across branch pages, and use a locality-specific filename or label only after obtaining location evidence.

## Centre façade verification

Use distinctive building features matched to the actual branch's Google profile photos as ownership evidence. A gallery label or matching phone alone is insufficient, especially when the visible address conflicts with the branch's locality or postcode.

**Why:** A Kalwa-labelled façade had a matching contact number but signage naming Kopri/Thane East, which contradicted the Kalwa address. Conversely, the Manpada profile's exterior photo matched the local building and entrance even though its façade sign said “Aggarwal Heritage” and its listed address said “Aggarwal Arcade.”

**How to apply:** A direct profile-photo building match can verify the branch owner without proving that two building names are interchangeable. Report such naming discrepancies explicitly. If ownership is not verified and the user requests a fallback, retain the current hero and neutral alt text rather than assigning the candidate a centre-specific claim.
