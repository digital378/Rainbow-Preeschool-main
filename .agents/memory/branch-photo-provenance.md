name: Branch photo provenance
description: Label centre-page photos by verified origin, not filename or gallery context.
---

Do not identify a photo as taken at a specific centre unless its location is independently verified. A real Rainbow Preschool image may be suitable for a centre page without proving it was photographed at that centre; use location-neutral descriptions and a "Thane photo" classification when provenance is unknown.

**Why:** Photos selected for the Kalwa branch page were real Rainbow photos, but none came with evidence tying them to Kalwa. Naming them as Kalwa photos would make an unsupported claim even if their content and alt text were otherwise accurate.

**How to apply:** Check the visible image subject before writing alt text, confirm content-hash exclusivity across branch pages, and use a locality-specific filename or label only after obtaining location evidence.
