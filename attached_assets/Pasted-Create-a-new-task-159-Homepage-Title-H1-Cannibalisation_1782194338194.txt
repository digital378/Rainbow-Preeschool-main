------------------------------------------------------------

- URL

- whether anchor is repeated elsewhere on homepage
- action: keep, rewrite, reduce, or remove

Allowed:
- One clear footer/navigation link to correct landing page.

Risky:
- Same keyword anchor repeated in footer, body, Explore More block, CTA and schema.

- Link labels that use exact-match keywords where a brand-safe label would work better.

Do not remove useful navigation unnecessarily. Reduce repetition and keep link equity clean.
