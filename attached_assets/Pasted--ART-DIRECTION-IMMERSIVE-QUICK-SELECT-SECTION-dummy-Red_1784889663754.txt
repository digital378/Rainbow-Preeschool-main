# ART DIRECTION — IMMERSIVE QUICK-SELECT SECTION (/dummy)
Redesign the 6-tile quick-select row (Why Us · Find Centre · Book Visit ·
Playgroup · Nursery · Kindergarten) into a playful, modern, 3D "Rainbow Shelf".
Match the hero's look (cream bg, pastel aurora, red #EC210F brand accent,
Fredoka/Baloo headings + Plus Jakarta Sans body) and the same smooth-motion
rules (transform/opacity only, eased, 60fps, Lenis + GSAP).

## CONCEPT — "Rainbow Shelf of Toys"
The tiles sit on the cloud divider like colourful toys lined up on a shelf.
Each tile is a soft 3D-tilting card with ONE chunky 3D icon floating above its
surface (real parallax depth), its own brand-spectrum colour, and a signature
playful animation on hover. The row reads as a rainbow left→right.

## STRUCTURE (group + label the two categories)
Add a small playful section heading centered above the row:
  eyebrow: "WHERE TO NEXT?"  (uppercase, letter-spacing, --ink-soft)
  title:   "Start Exploring"  (Fredoka, --ink)
Then TWO labelled groups on one row (divider/gap between them):
  GROUP A — "Quick Links":  Why Us · Find Centre · Book Visit
  GROUP B — "Our Programmes":  Playgroup · Nursery · Kindergarten
On the 3 programme cards add a small age-band tag ONLY if verified — otherwise
use a "[verify age]" placeholder, do NOT invent ranges.

## PER-TILE COLOUR + 3D ICON + SIGNATURE HOVER MOTION
Keep each tile's existing colour, upgraded to a rich gradient + soft coloured

## CARD DESIGN (exact)
- Soft white card, radius 22px, 1px hairline, base shadow
  0 10px 30px rgba(33,27,46,0.08). Generous padding, icon centered top,
  label below (Fredoka 600, the card's colour), and a tiny "→" that's hidden
  until hover.
- Idle: card rests flat; icon bobs slowly (sine, phase-varied per card).
- The card's colour lives as a faint radial glow behind the icon + a 2px
  gradient top-border accent.

## INTERACTIONS (the "wow")
1. 3D TILT: card tilts toward the cursor (rotateX/Y, max ~10°, perspective
   ~800px), icon parallax-shifts opposite for depth. Damped, springy return.
2. LIFT: on hover the card rises 10px off the shelf, shadow grows + tints to
   the card colour, coloured aura blooms, the "→" slides in, and the icon runs
   its signature animation (above).
3. MAGNETIC: the icon (and card) gently pull ~6px toward the cursor.
4. SELECT/ACTIVE state: clicked/active card gets a coloured ring + filled
   colour chip; others dim slightly (focus the choice).
5. SCROLL ENTRANCE: as the section enters, tiles POP UP one-by-one from behind
   the cloud (stagger 70ms, back-out ease) like toys being placed on the shelf.
   Group labels fade/slide in first.
6. CURSOR: soft sparkle on hover; on click a tiny confetti/star burst in the
   card's colour.

## MOBILE
- Horizontal snap-scroll carousel (peek the next card) OR 2-column grid — pick
  whichever keeps tap targets ≥44px. Keep the 3D icons, bob, tap-to-animate,
  and coloured auras. Tilt driven by touch/gyro. Snap has soft momentum (Lenis).
- Group labels stack above their sets. Buttery 60fps; reduce shadows/blur.

## PERFORMANCE & ACCESSIBILITY
- Real semantic <a>/<button> links with clear focus rings (keyboard + screen

- transform/opacity only; will-change on hover; icons preloaded; respect
  prefers-reduced-motion (static tiles, colour + icon, no bob/tilt).
