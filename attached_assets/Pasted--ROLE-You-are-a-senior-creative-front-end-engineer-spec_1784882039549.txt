# THE 3D / IMMERSIVE DIRECTION (this is the heart of the task)
Build a dreamy, playful "world of wonder" 3D hero — the feeling of a child's
imagination. Concept: a soft, sunlit floating world.

1. **3D scene (R3F canvas as the hero background):**
   - Soft volumetric clouds / gradient sky using drei (`<Cloud/>`, `<Sky/>`
     or a custom shader gradient in RPS warm tones).
   - Floating, gently-bobbing low-poly playful props scattered in 3D depth:
     ABC alphabet blocks, balloons, crayons, stars, a paper plane, a small
     rocket, building blocks, and a soft rainbow arc. Use instancing where
     possible. Gentle sine-wave float + slow rotation on each.
   - Subtle bloom + depth-of-field via postprocessing (keep it tasteful,
     performance-safe).

2. **The child image → make it feel 3D (2.5D depth parallax):**
   - Take the hero child photo and render it as a depth-displaced plane:
     use the photo as the color texture + a generated depth map to displace
     it, so moving the mouse gives a real parallax "pop-out" 3D effect.
   - The floating glass rating card tilts in 3D on hover (react to cursor,
     glassmorphism, soft shadow).
   - (Leave a clearly-commented slot `// TODO: swap plane for GLB model`
     so we can later drop in an actual glTF 3D child/school model.)

3. **Mouse interactions:**
   - Whole scene has camera/parallax reaction to mouse position (subtle,
     springy, damped — not jittery).
   - Custom playful cursor with a soft sparkle/trail (respects reduced motion).
   - Props nearest the cursor drift slightly away / react.

4. **Scroll-driven storytelling (GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis):**
   - On load: staggered reveal of badge → H1 (per-word, with the gold
     gradient sweeping across "Preschool") → subhead → description →
     chips (pop in one by one) → CTAs → card floats up.
   - On scroll: camera pushes gently INTO the scene, clouds part, the
     rainbow arc draws/forms, props parallax at different depths, and the
     hero text pins briefly then releases into the next section.
   - The "Scroll to explore" indicator animates and fades as user scrolls.

5. **Polish:**
   - Animated gradient / aurora glow behind the H1.
   - Magnetic buttons (CTA subtly pulls toward cursor).
   - Chips and card have soft 3D hover lift.
