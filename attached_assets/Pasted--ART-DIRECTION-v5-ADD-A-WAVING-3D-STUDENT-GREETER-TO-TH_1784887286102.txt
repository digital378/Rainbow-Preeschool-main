## MOTION GRAPHICS — the character must feel ALIVE (GSAP timelines)
Run everything in a single rAF/GSAP timeline; transform+opacity only; eased,
never linear. Layer these looping motions with varied timing so it never looks
robotic:
1. ENTRANCE (on load): character rises up + scales from 0.9→1 with a soft
   back-out (cubic-bezier .34,1.56,.64,1), contact shadow fades in, then it
   does its first big wave. ~1s, delightful.
2. IDLE BREATHING: subtle vertical bob + tiny scaleY (1→1.015→1), 3.5s sine,
   infinite. This is the base "aliveness."
3. SWAY: gentle whole-body rotation ±1.5° and x-shift ±4px, ~5s, offset phase
   from the breathe so it feels organic.
4. BLINK: swap eyes-open→closed for ~120ms every 4–7s (randomized interval).
   If no eye layers, fake with a thin scaleY squash on a head mask.
5. WAVE (the hero move): rotate `student-arm` around the SHOULDER pivot
   (transform-origin at the shoulder joint) from rest → ~35° and back, 3 quick
   friendly oscillations with easeInOut, then rest. Auto-plays every ~6s AND on
   hover/tap. Add a tiny head-tilt toward the raised arm during the wave.
6. Optional: a small "Hi!" speech bubble pops near the head during the wave
   (scale-in with back-out, fades after 1.2s).
