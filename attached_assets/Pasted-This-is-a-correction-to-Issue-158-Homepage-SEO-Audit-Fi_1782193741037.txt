------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO HANDLE FOUND PHRASES
------------------------------------------------------------

Do not blindly remove every phrase. Classify each occurrence:

1. Allowed occurrence
Allowed if:
- It is a navigation link pointing to the correct landing page.
- It appears once in a compact link list.
- It is not used as H1, H2, repeated body text, schema headline, or meta title.

Example allowed:
Anchor text "Best Preschool in Thane" linking to /best-preschool-near-me-in-thane

2. Risky occurrence
Risky if:
- It appears in H1/H2/H3.
- It appears in homepage title.
- It appears repeatedly in body copy.
- It appears in schema name/headline/description.
- It appears in FAQ questions as a homepage topic.
- It appears in image alt text unnecessarily.
- It appears in multiple homepage sections.

3. Required rewrite
Rewrite risky homepage occurrences into brand-safe variants.

Use these homepage-safe alternatives:
- "preschool chain in Thane"
- "early years programmes across Thane"
- "six Rainbow Preschool centres in Thane"
- "trusted preschool network since 2007"
- "play-based early learning"
- "programmes for young learners"
- "book a campus visit"
- "admissions support team"

Avoid making homepage copy target a single landing-page keyword.
