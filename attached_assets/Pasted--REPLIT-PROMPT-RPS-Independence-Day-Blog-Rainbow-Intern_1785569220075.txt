## PART 3 — CURSOR ANIMATION (Patriotic Star-Sparkle Trail)

Add a custom cursor animation: a trail of tiny saffron, white, and green glitter stars that appear at the cursor position and fade out over 800ms. This suits the playful, joyful preschool brand perfectly.

```javascript
// PATRIOTIC STAR-SPARKLE CURSOR TRAIL
(function() {
  const colors = ['#FF9933', '#FFFFFF', '#138808', '#F0A820', '#EC210F'];
  const stars = ['★', '✦', '✧', '✶', '·'];

  document.addEventListener('mousemove', function(e) {
    const sparkle = document.createElement('span');
    sparkle.className = 'cursor-sparkle';
    sparkle.textContent = stars[Math.floor(Math.random() * stars.length)];
    sparkle.style.cssText = `
      position: fixed;
      left: ${e.clientX}px;
      top: ${e.clientY}px;
      pointer-events: none;
      z-index: 9999;
      font-size: ${10 + Math.random() * 14}px;
      color: ${colors[Math.floor(Math.random() * colors.length)]};
      transform: translate(-50%, -50%);
      animation: sparkle-fade 0.8s ease-out forwards;
      text-shadow: 0 0 4px currentColor;
    `;
    document.body.appendChild(sparkle);
    setTimeout(() => sparkle.remove(), 800);
  });
})();
```

Add this CSS keyframe:
```css
@keyframes sparkle-fade {
  0%   { opacity: 1; transform: translate(-50%, -50%) scale(1) rotate(0deg); }
  50%  { opacity: 0.7; transform: translate(-50%, -120%) scale(1.3) rotate(20deg); }
  100% { opacity: 0; transform: translate(-50%, -200%) scale(0.3) rotate(45deg); }
}
```

Also add `cursor: none;` on `body` and create a custom cursor dot:
```css
body { cursor: none; }
.custom-cursor {
  width: 18px; height: 18px;
  background: var(--rps-red);
  border: 2px solid var(--saffron);
  border-radius: 50%;
  position: fixed;
  pointer-events: none;
  z-index: 10000;
  transition: transform 0.1s ease;
}
```
Track cursor position with JS and move the `.custom-cursor` div accordingly. On mobile (touch device), disable both cursor effects.

**FAQ 1 — How do I explain Independence Day to a 3-year-old?**
Tell your child: "Long ago, India was ruled by people from a faraway country called Britain. Some very brave and kind people in India — like Gandhiji — wanted everyone to be free. **FAQ 2 — What is a good Independence Day activity for preschoolers at home?**
Some easy, joyful activities include: making a paper tricolor flag together, drawing India's map and colouring it, watching a short animated video about freedom fighters, singing the national anthem, or doing a simple fancy dress as a farmer, soldier, doctor, or teacher to represent different people who serve India.

**FAQ 3 — Why does Independence Day fall on 15th August?**
On 15th August 1947, at the stroke of midnight, India officially became a free and independent nation. The British handed over control to Indian leaders, and Jawaharlal Nehru — India's first Prime Minister — gave his famous "Tryst with Destiny" speech. This is why 15th August is celebrated as Independence Day every year.

**FAQ 4 — What is the difference between Independence Day and Republic Day?**
Independence Day (15th August) celebrates the day India became free from British rule in 1947. Republic Day (26th January) celebrates the day India's own Constitution came into effect in 1950 — the day India became a fully self-governing democratic republic. Both are national holidays celebrated with flag hoisting, parades, and patriotic events.

**FAQ 5 — Who were India's most important freedom fighters?**
India had thousands of freedom fighters! Some of the most well-known include: Mahatma Gandhi (non-violence and civil disobedience), Jawaharlal Nehru (first PM), Rani Lakshmibai (warrior queen of Jhansi), Bhagat Singh (revolutionary martyr), Subhas Chandra Bose (INA leader), Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (Constitution's architect), Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel (unified India), and Bal Gangadhar Tilak. There were millions of ordinary Indians who participated in the struggle too.

**FAQ 6 — How should preschools celebrate Independence Day?**
Schools can make it meaningful and fun through: flag hoisting ceremonies with age-appropriate explanations, patriotic songs and rhymes, simple fancy dress events, colouring sheets of the national flag or map, short storytelling about freedom fighters, making paper flags or crafts, and a special snack in tricolor (saffron ladoo / white coconut / green chutney!).
