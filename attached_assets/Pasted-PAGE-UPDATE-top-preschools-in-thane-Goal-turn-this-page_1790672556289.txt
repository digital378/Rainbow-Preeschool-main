WHY WE'RE CHANGING THIS PAGE
The page is published by Rainbow but reads as an independent review ("We evaluated 50+ preschools…"). It also lists weaknesses for other schools that we can't prove. That is a trust risk and a legal risk. Google also treats this kind of self-ranked "review" page as low quality. A clearly labelled, fact-only comparison is safer and more useful to parents. It is also easier for Google and AI answers to quote, because every line is a checkable fact.
