hue, weight 700. Add a 3px vivid top-accent bar and a faint (6–8%) wash of the
hue at the card's bottom so each card clearly "owns" its colour. Use these
saturated gradients:
- Why Us       → #F5320C → #FF5A3C   (brand red, lead)
- Find Centre  → #06B463 → #22D67E
- Book Visit   → #1F7AF0 → #48A0FF

- Nursery      → #7C4DFF → #A06BFF
- Kindergarten → #06B6A4 → #2CD8C4
Icon chip ~60–64px, radius 18px, coloured drop-shadow (same hue, 30% alpha).
On hover the chip saturates further + the glow blooms. Result: the row should
read as a bright, confident rainbow — not pastel and faint.
