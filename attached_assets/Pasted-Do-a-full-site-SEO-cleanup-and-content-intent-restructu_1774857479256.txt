SITE-WIDE SEO CLEANUP TASKS

1. CRAWL AND CLASSIFY ALL MAIN WEBSITE PAGES
Audit every indexable page and classify pages into groups such as:
- homepage
- about
- programme pages
- admissions page
- nearby-intent pages
- “best preschool”/comparison pages
- centre/location pages
- gallery/contact/utility pages
- blogs/news/articles
- branded authority pages

For each page identify:
- page purpose
- likely primary keyword intent
- secondary keyword themes
- overlap risk
- whether the page is commercial, local, navigational, or informational

2. CREATE A KEYWORD OWNERSHIP MAP
Assign one clear primary search intent to each major commercial and SEO page so they stop competing against each other.

Examples of ownership logic to implement:
- /best-preschool-near-me-in-thane should own “best preschool in thane” and close variants
- /preschool-near-me should own nearby-centre / nearest preschool intent
- /preschool-admissions should own admission-intent queries
- /playgroup should own broad programme intent
- /playgroup-in-thane should own city-level playgroup local intent if retained
- centre pages should own locality-specific preschool intent
- support/blog pages should reinforce commercial pages, not outrank them accidentally

Implement this mapping through content, metadata, headings, and internal linking.

3. FIX KEYWORD CANNIBALIZATION
Review all pages for overlapping keyword targets and clean them up.

Important overlap areas likely include:
- best preschool vs near me vs play school near me
- admissions pages vs admission guide content
- programme pages vs city-level programme pages vs locality-level programme pages
- multiple informational articles covering nearly identical topics
- branded methodology/safety/approach pages that may overlap each other

For each overlap case, fix using one or more of:
- sharpening page positioning
- revising headings and copy direction
- changing metadata
- changing internal linking targets
- adjusting keyword emphasis
- making support pages more informational and less commercial
- reducing overlap in hero/H1/title copy
- consolidating or de-prioritizing pages only if truly necessary

Review all indexable pages and make metadata unique, intent-specific, and non-duplicative.

Ensure:

Fix heading hierarchy site-wide.

Ensure:

- H2/H3 support the page’s actual keyword intent
- headings do not copy the same phrasing across overlapping pages
- page sections reinforce differentiated search purpose

8. INTERNAL LINKING RESTRUCTURE
Update internal linking so it supports page hierarchy correctly.

Goals:

- centre pages should link to relevant programme/admissions pages
- “best preschool” pages and “near me” pages should not cannibalize each other through mixed signals
