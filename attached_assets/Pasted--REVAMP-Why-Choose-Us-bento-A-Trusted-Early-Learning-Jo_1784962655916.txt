## STEP 3 — IMMERSIVE INTERACTION (per tile; transform/opacity only, 60fps)
Wrap all in: if (!matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion:reduce)').matches){…}
1. SPOTLIGHT: on pointermove, update CSS vars --mx/--my to cursor position; paint a
   radial-gradient glow in the tile's theme colour (~18% opacity, ~180px) that
   follows the cursor. Fades out on pointerleave.
2. 3D TILT: perspective:1000px on the grid; each tile transform-style:preserve-3d;
   rotateX/rotateY ±8° driven by pointer position (spring/damped return). The 3D
   icon sits on a higher translateZ so it pops toward the viewer as the tile tilts.
3. ICON LIFE: icon bobs slowly at idle (translateY ±5px, 4s sine, phase-varied per
   tile); on hover it does a small pop + 8° spin, and the tile lifts translateY(-6px)
   with a theme-tinted shadow bloom.
4. ENTRANCE: tiles fade + rise + slight 3D flip on scroll-in, staggered 80ms
   (hero card first). Use the section container as the ScrollTrigger scope.
