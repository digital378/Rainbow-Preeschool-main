Accessibility issues (lower priority but easy fixes)
Your accessibility score is 89 — close to the 90 threshold for "good." Four flags:

Prohibited ARIA attributes — usually aria-* on elements that don't support them (e.g. aria-hidden on a focusable element). 5-minute fix.
Insufficient color contrast — likely text on the colored hero or buttons. Use a contrast checker (webaim.org) on the flagged elements.
Touch targets too small — buttons or links smaller than 48×48 px. Common in dense footers.
Identical links have the same purpose — likely duplicate "Read more" or "Enquire" links without aria-label distinction.
